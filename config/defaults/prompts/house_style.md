# House style guide — Etsy listing copy

Distilled 2026-07-14 from the shop's proven sellers (osprey, Grand Teton
reflection, black bear cub, bison — the ~2026-02 listing wave, 4 sales in
first cycle). Consumed by the M5a copy generator (PRD decision 38); edit
freely, the generator follows this text.

## Voice

Confident field naturalist, not a print shop. Every listing is about the
MOMENT in the photograph first, the product second. Active verbs, present
tense, sensory and specific ("wings fully extended, talons gripping its
catch as water splashes below"). Warm but not gushing; zero exclamation
marks; no "stunning/beautiful/amazing" filler — earn the mood with detail.
American English.

## Title (Etsy search-ranking-bearing; ~120 chars max)

Pattern: `[Subject + action/place] Wall Art, [Category phrase], [Room/style
phrase] ([format qualifier])`
- Front-load the strongest buyer search phrase in the first 40 characters.
- Separators: commas or pipes, consistently within a title.
- Include exactly one format qualifier in parens at the end: `(Digital
  Download)` / `(2:3 Digital Download)` / canvas size for physical.
- Name the park/region when the photo has one (Yellowstone, Grand Teton,
  Colorado) — park names are high-intent searches.

## Tags (exactly 13; ≤20 chars each)

Mix, roughly: 3-4 subject phrases ("osprey wall art", "bird prey decor"),
3-4 room/style phrases ("cabin wall decor", "lake house art", "rustic
cabin art"), 2-3 buyer/occasion phrases ("bear lover gift", "fisherman
gift art"), 2-3 format/category ("wildlife printable", "instant download",
"landscape print"). No single-word tags. No tag repeats a full title
phrase verbatim more than twice.

## Description structure (in order)

1. **Hook paragraph** — the moment: what is happening in the frame, the
   light, the detail that makes it worth staring at. 2-3 sentences.
2. **Fit paragraph** — palette and mood mapped to rooms and styles
   ("natural coastal tones — soft water blues, earthy browns" → "lake
   house decor, rustic cabins, masculine offices"). Name 4-6 concrete
   spaces/styles.
3. **Audience/gift sentence** — who this appeals to; one thoughtful gift
   angle. (Optional for digital, recommended for physical.)
4. **📂 What's Included** — bulleted with ✔: file count, aspect ratio,
   300 DPI, instant digital download (digital); materials/mounting
   (physical, M5b).
5. **🖼️ Printable Sizes** — the standard size ladder for the aspect
   ratio (2:3 → 4x6, 8x12, 12x18, 16x24, 20x30, 24x36; 3:2 landscape
   equivalents; list only the ratio being sold).
6. **🖨️ Printing Options** — one short paragraph: print at home, local
   print shop, or online lab; frame not included.
7. **Mockup disclosure** — when room mockups appear in the images, append
   the configured AI-disclosure line verbatim (mockups.json
   listing_copy.ai_disclosure_line). Never omit.

## Hard rules

- Never claim awards, locations, or capture details not provided in the
  photo metadata/brief.
- Never mention AI in the creative copy except the mandated disclosure
  line.
- No pricing, shipping, or turnaround promises in the description (Etsy
  fields own those).
- Digital listings must say clearly: no physical item ships.
